Each simulation costs **1 credit**. The **Credits** number in the simulator
sidebar is your total across all sources.

## Credits are grouped by source

Your credits aren't one single pot — they are kept per source:

- **Personal credits** — yours alone. Runs paid from them are **Private**:
  nobody but you ever sees the transcript or evaluation.
- **Group credits** — one bucket per seminar group you've joined. Runs paid
  from them are **Shared**: the group's teacher sees the transcript and
  evaluation.

When you start a simulation and belong to at least one group, the **Run as**
step lets you choose which source pays — and therefore who sees the run. See
[Starting a simulation](/en/simulator/starting-a-simulation).

## Where credits come from

| Source                  | What you get                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Joining a seminar group | A one-time grant of the group's per-student amount, into that group's bucket. See [Joining a group](/en/simulator/joining-a-group). |
| Your group's teacher    | The organizer can add more credits to your group bucket at any time.                                                                |
| The Edustories team     | Personal credits granted directly to your account.                                                                                  |

Credits can't be purchased in the app. If you run out, the simulator shows
"You have no credits. Ask your seminar organizer to grant you some." If you're
not part of a group, reach out to the Edustories team.

## When a credit is spent (and when it's returned)

- A credit is deducted when the simulation **starts**.
- If the simulation fails to start (a technical error before the pupil's first
  message), the credit is returned automatically.
- An **Abandoned** simulation (left inactive for 2 hours) does **not** return
  its credit — the run happened, it just was never ended. Always finish with
  **End conversation** to get your evaluation. See
  [Results and history](/en/simulator/results-and-history).
