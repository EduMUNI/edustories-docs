---
title: Seminar groups
description: For teachers and lecturers — create a group, invite students with a link or QR code, and review every attempt.
---

Seminar groups turn the simulator into a classroom tool. You create a group,
share a join link, and every student who joins receives credits to run
simulations. You then see each student's status, transcript, and evaluation in
one dashboard — including students who run it **without an account** (guests).

<Callout title="Group creation is enabled on request">
  Creating groups requires organizer access granted by the Edustories team. Once
  enabled, a **Manage groups** link appears in your simulator sidebar. Joining
  and running simulations in groups needs no special access.
</Callout>

## Creating a group

In **Manage groups**, press **Create group** and fill in:

- **Name** — how the group appears to you and to joining students (e.g.
  "Special pedagogy, Tuesday seminar").
- **Credits granted per student on join** — how many simulations each student
  gets when they join. For guests (students without an account), this is also
  the maximum number of attempts per device.
- **Enabled languages** — which simulation languages (Czech, English) members
  of this group may use.

<Callout type="warn" title="Settings are fixed after creation">
  A group's name, per-student credits, and languages can't be changed later. You
  can still add extra credits to individual students at any time.
</Callout>

## Inviting students

Every group has a **Join link** card with:

- the invite **URL** and a **Copy link** button,
- a **QR code** for scanning in class,
- **Present mode** — a fullscreen QR code to project, with a live counter of
  how many students have joined (no names are shown on screen).

Anyone with the link can join while the group is open — share it only with
your class. What students see when they open it is described in
[Joining a group](/en/simulator/joining-a-group).

## The group dashboard

The dashboard refreshes automatically every few seconds, so you can leave it
open during a seminar and watch progress live.

### Students

One row per member with their status — **Joined** (hasn't started yet),
**In simulation**, or **Finished** — the number of completed attempts, and the
credits they've been granted. Guests are marked with a **Guest** badge and
appear under the name or ID they entered.

Row actions:

- **View transcript** — opens a finished attempt with the same
  **Results** / **Conversation** tabs the student sees. Works for guests too.
- **Add credits** — grant more runs to a student (+1, +3, +5, +10). Available
  for account members only; guests are limited to the group's per-student
  amount.
- **Remove** — removes the member from the group. This takes effect
  immediately, without a confirmation prompt.

### All attempts

A chronological list of every simulation run in the group, including guests'
runs, with its status and a link to the transcript and evaluation of each
finished attempt. Attempts by members who later deleted their account remain
listed.

## Closing a group

**Close group** stops new students from joining — the invite link shows "This
group is closed to new members." Closing also stops guests from starting
further runs (guests start each run from the invite page). Students with
accounts who already joined can keep using their remaining credits, and you
keep full access to all transcripts. **Reopen** makes the link work again.

<Callout title="Tell your students about visibility">
  Students are shown a notice before joining: by joining, their transcripts and
  evaluations become visible to you. Runs they start outside the group (as
  **Personal**) stay private to them.
</Callout>
