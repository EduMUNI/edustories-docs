## The interview evaluation

After you end a simulation, the results page opens with two tabs: **Results**
(the evaluation) and **Conversation** (the full transcript).

The **Interview evaluation** is a structured written assessment of your
interview. It covers:

| Section               | What it tells you                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recognized profile    | What the pupil was actually dealing with. For a random profile, this is where you find out which profile the AI played.                                         |
| Evidence              | Concrete moments from your interview that pointed to the profile.                                                                                               |
| Discovered vs. missed | How much you uncovered across six areas: school performance, behavior in class, the home situation, peer relationships, triggers, and what helps the pupil.     |
| Problematic moments   | Quotes of your questions or reactions that could have gone better (leading questions, labeling, dismissing feelings) — or a note that there were no major ones. |
| Feedback              | Two things you did well, two recommendations, and three examples of better open questions you could have asked.                                                 |

Switch to the **Conversation** tab to re-read the whole transcript next to the
feedback. Press **New simulation** to immediately start another run.

## My simulations

The sidebar section **My simulations** lists all your past runs, each with its
profile and a status badge:

| Status          | Meaning                                                                                                                  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **In progress** | The conversation hasn't been ended. Click it to resume exactly where you left off — the full chat history loads back in. |
| **Finished**    | Evaluated. Click it to reopen the results and transcript — they are kept permanently.                                    |
| **Abandoned**   | Started but left inactive for 2 hours. It cannot be reopened or evaluated, and the credit is not returned.               |

<Callout title="On smaller screens">
  Below desktop width the sidebar is hidden. Use the bar under the page header
  to open it — your credits and simulation history are in there.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Group simulations are shared">
  Runs you start with **Run as** set to a seminar group also appear to that
  group's teacher, including the transcript and evaluation. Runs marked
  **Private** are yours alone. See [Starting a
  simulation](/en/simulator/starting-a-simulation).
</Callout>
