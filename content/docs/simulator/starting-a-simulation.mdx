---
title: Starting a simulation
description: Choose a student profile, set the pupil's grade and language, and pick which credits pay for the run.
---

Open the simulator from the user menu (**Diagnostic simulator**) and press
**New simulation**. A short wizard walks you through the setup.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Select student profile

Pick the difficulty or life situation the AI pupil will portray — for example
a specific learning difficulty, a problem at home, or a relationship issue in
class. Each profile card carries a short description of what the pupil is
going through.

- Use the **Search a profile…** box to filter the grid. The search ignores
  diacritics, so typing `reseni` also matches `řešení`.
- The last card is the **random profile** (🎲): the AI picks the profile, the
  pupil's age, and the concrete situation for you. You only find out what the
  pupil was dealing with in the final evaluation — the closest thing to a real
  blind interview.

</Step>
<Step>

### About the pupil

For a specific (non-random) profile, choose the pupil's **grade** (1st through
9th grade, each shown with the typical age). The AI adapts the pupil's
vocabulary and behavior to that age.

If more than one **simulation language** is available, you also pick the
language here (Czech or English). When you run the simulation inside a seminar
group, the choice is limited to the languages the group has enabled.

<Callout title="This step can be skipped">
  If you picked the random profile and only one language is available, there is
  nothing to choose — the wizard jumps straight to the review step.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Review & start

The last step summarizes your choices and offers two optional settings:

- **Lesson context** — a free-text note for the AI, e.g. "the interview takes
  place right after a math test the pupil failed". Use it to anchor the
  conversation in a concrete moment.
- **Run as** — shown only when you are a member of at least one seminar group.
  Your credits can come from different sources, and this choice decides which
  ones pay for the run and who sees it:
  - **Personal** (badge **Private**): "Just for you — paid from your personal
    credits." Nobody else sees the transcript.
  - A **group** (badge **Shared**): paid from that group's credits, and the
    group's teacher will see the transcript and evaluation.

  Each option shows how many credits remain in that source. Options with no
  credits left, or groups that don't allow your selected language, are
  disabled. The first usable option is pre-selected.

Press **Start simulation** to begin. The button stays disabled until you have
a profile, a grade (for non-random profiles), and a usable credit source.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="No credits?">
  Starting a simulation costs 1 credit. If you have none, the simulator shows
  "You have no credits. Ask your seminar organizer to grant you some." — see
  [Credits](/en/simulator/credits) for where credits come from.
</Callout>
