---
title: During a simulation
description: How the interview chat works, what the AI pupil will and won't do, and how to end the conversation.
---

A simulation is a chat between you and the AI pupil. The pupil speaks first —
typically a hesitant opening line, the way a child called in for a talk might
actually start. From there, you lead the interview.

## The chat

- Type your message into the box ("Write a message to the student...") and
  press **Send**. **Enter** sends the message; **Shift+Enter** inserts a new
  line.
- While the pupil is "thinking", a typing indicator appears. Wait for the
  reply before planning your next question.
- There is no hard limit on length or number of turns. The interview is
  designed for roughly five to eight minutes; if it runs long, the pupil may
  naturally signal being tired or wanting to go back to class — just like a
  real child would.

## What the pupil will and won't do

The AI stays in character as a child of the selected age with the selected
profile, for the whole conversation:

- It answers the way a real pupil might: briefly, sometimes evasively, opening
  up only when your questions make it feel safe to.
- It portrays **one** profile per simulation. If you try to steer the
  conversation toward a different difficulty than the one being played, the AI
  breaks character and points it out rather than improvising a second problem.
- It won't hand you the diagnosis. Your job is to uncover the situation
  through open questions, not to get the pupil to name it.

## Ending the conversation

When you have learned what you can (or want to see how you did), press
**End conversation** and confirm. The AI then writes the **Interview
evaluation** — this can take a little while, up to a couple of minutes for a
long transcript. Once it's ready, you land on the results page.

<Callout title="Leaving without ending">
  If you close the tab or navigate away without ending the conversation, the
  simulation stays **In progress** and you can resume it anytime from **My
  simulations** in the sidebar. However, after **2 hours without a message** the
  simulation is marked **Abandoned**: it can no longer be opened or evaluated,
  and the credit is not returned. If you want the evaluation, always finish with
  **End conversation**.
</Callout>
