---
title: Scores, endings & replay
description: Understand how your EduPlay score works, the three possible endings, and how to replay a case to try a different approach.
---

Every EduPlay session tracks a single **Score** that summarises how well your
decisions are going, and that score decides which ending you reach.

## How the score works

Your score runs from **0 to 100** and **starts at 50**. After every decision it
updates: good choices push it up, poor or harsh choices pull it down. The score is
always kept within the 0–100 range.

There is a built-in **resilience cushion**. If you've been doing well and built up
a high score, the damage from a single bad choice is softened — a strong
performance buffers one mistake instead of letting it sink you completely. This
mirrors how genuine trust earned with a student gives you a little room to slip.

The same score drives the **Decision timeline** chart you see during the play,
which runs from **Crisis** at the bottom, through **Middle**, to **Resolution** at
the top.

<Callout type="warn">
  EduPlay scoring is **completely separate from Training**. Your EduPlay score,
  the resilience cushion, and the ending you reach all live inside that single
  play. Playing a case does **not** affect your Training skills, your points, or
  your position on the Training leaderboard. EduPlay is a stand-alone reflective
  exercise — the reward is the ending and what you learn from it.
</Callout>

## The three endings

After the final decision, your play resolves to one of three endings, chosen by
your **final score**:

| Ending              | When it fires      | What it means                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Success** (green) | High final score   | A successful intervention — you turned the situation around |
| **Warning** (amber) | Middle final score | You got through it, but without real growth                 |
| **Danger** (red)    | Low final score    | A crisis outcome                                            |

## What the ending screen shows

The ending screen reflects on how your play went:

- **A title and message** — an AI-written headline and a short reflection,
  matched in tone to the ending you reached.
- **Final score** — labelled **"Final score"**, shown as a coloured bar on a
  scale from **Crisis** to **Resolution**.
- **The full decision timeline** — the same chart from the play, now complete.
- **Key moments** — your three most impactful decisions, each shown as a card
  with the scene, the choice you made, how much it moved your score, and a short
  pedagogical insight about that move.
- **"What you've learned"** — a short list of takeaway learning points written
  for the ending you reached.

## Play again

At the bottom of the ending screen, **Play again** starts a fresh session on the
same case report — the heart of the "rewind and try a different approach" loop.
Because the choices genuinely branch, a replay can take you down a different path
to a different ending. **Back to case** returns you to the case report's detail
page.
