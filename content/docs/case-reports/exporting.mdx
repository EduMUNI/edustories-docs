---
title: Exporting a case to Word (.docx)
description: Download a case report as a Word document, choosing which sections to include and what to name the file.
---

You can save any case report as a Word document (`.docx`) to keep, print, or adapt for your own use. The export is built from the **Download** button on a case's detail page.

<Callout type="info">You need to be logged in to export a case.</Callout>

## How to export

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the case and click Download

On the case's detail page, click the **Download** button. The **"Download to .docx"** dialog opens.

</Step>

<Step>
### Name your file

Enter a name for the document. This is what the downloaded `.docx` file will be called.

</Step>

<Step>
### Choose which sections to include

Tick the parts of the case you want in the document. The options are grouped:

- **Case Description** — Summary, Description, and Anamnesis (the student's background).
- **User solution** — the educator's own solution and its outcome.
- **AI Solution** — any of the AI approaches: Proactive, Reactive, PBIS, SEL, RP, and NVC.

Include only what you need — for example, just the description and one AI approach.

</Step>

<Step>
### Download

Confirm to generate and download your `.docx` file.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- The document is produced in your **current interface language**, so set your language before exporting if you want a specific one.
- The result is an ordinary Word file you can open, edit, and share like any other document.

<Callout type="info">
  Formal education plans (BIP, IEP, PLPP) are described on the [Education
  plans](/en/case-reports/education-plans) page; the AI Solution options here
  are the lens-based approaches covered under [AI-recommended
  solutions](/en/case-reports/ai-solutions).
</Callout>
