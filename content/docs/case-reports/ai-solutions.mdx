Every case report includes an **"AI-powered solution designing"** section: a set of suggested approaches the AI drafts for the situation described. They give you practical, concrete ideas you can adapt to your own classroom.

## The approaches (lenses)

The AI looks at each case through several well-established lenses and presents them as tabs. Only the approaches it generated for a given case appear:

- **Proactive** — prevention-focused: steps to set the student up for success before problems arise.
- **Reactive** — in-the-moment: what to do when the challenging behaviour is actually happening.
- **SEL** — Social and Emotional Learning: building the student's emotional skills.
- **PBIS** — Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports: reinforcing positive behaviour systematically.
- **RP** — Restorative Practices: repairing relationships and harm rather than just punishing.
- **NVC** — Nonviolent Communication: a communication style that defuses conflict.

<Callout type="info">
  These are perspectives the system produces *for you* — you don't have to
  switch them on anywhere. They're also among the sections you can pick when you
  [export a case to Word](/en/case-reports/exporting).
</Callout>

## What's in each suggestion

For the Proactive and Reactive approaches, each suggested solution typically includes:

- A short **title** and description (sometimes with a **"Creative solution"** badge, marking a more inventive idea).
- A step-by-step **Scenario** — what to do, in order.
- A **Time plan** — how it fits over time.
- A **Reason** — the rationale behind it.
- **Indicators for successful behaviour** — signs to watch for that tell you it's working.

The other framework tabs (SEL, PBIS, RP, NVC) present their own structured guidance in the same spirit.

## Rate what's useful

Each section has its own **Like / Dislike** controls. Rating a suggestion opens a quick form with descriptive tags (such as _Practical_, _Inspirational_, _Useful_, or _Impractical_) so you can say _why_. Your feedback helps other educators see which approaches the community found most helpful.

## When solutions aren't there yet

You may sometimes see a **"Not generated"** badge or a message that the case is _still generating_. That means the AI hasn't finished producing the suggestions for that case yet — check back shortly. Some content also requires you to be logged in; if you're signed out you'll see a prompt to **log in to display** the full solutions.

## More formal plans
