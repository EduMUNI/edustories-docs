---
title: Editing your case report
description: Change the title, solution, outcome, and success rating of a case you've already created.
---

You can come back and update a case report after it's created. Open the case from your profile and choose to edit it — you'll land on the **"Edit Case"** screen.

## What you can edit

From the **"Edit Case"** screen you can change:

- **Title**
- **Solution** (your detailed solution description)
- **Outcome** (the result of your solution)
- **Success rating**

This is the place to add a solution you've since worked out, update an outcome as a situation develops, or correct the success rating.

## Save your changes

The buttons depend on whether your case is already published:

- If the case is **published**, you'll see **"Edit"**, which saves your changes and keeps it published.
- If the case is **private**, you'll see both **"Edit"** (saves and keeps it private) and **"Edit and publish"** (saves and shares it with the community).

<Callout type="info">
  The demographic and classification fields — age, sex/gender, frequency,
  country, diagnoses, and so on — are set when the case is first created and
  aren't changed from this screen. If you need different demographics, that's a
  sign it's really a different case.
</Callout>
