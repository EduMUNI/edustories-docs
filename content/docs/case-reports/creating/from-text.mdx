---
title: Creating by typing or pasting
description: Fill in the case report form yourself with full control over the wording, and let the AI add a title and summary.
---

If you'd rather write your case out (or paste in notes you already have), choose **"Fill in writing"** when you start a case report. This gives you a single form with everything in one place.

## The form

The text form has a few parts:

- **Required Data** and **Optional Data** — the same structured fields about the student as the voice path: age, sex/gender, the frequency of the challenging behaviour, and country are required; family status, disorders, severity, diagnoses, hobbies, and grades are optional.
- **"Detailed description of the situation (behavior level)"** — the long narrative of what happened. This needs at least **100 characters** (a live counter shows your progress).
- **"Student's anamnesis"** — the student's background and history. This also needs at least **100 characters**.

<Callout type="info">
  The 100-character minimums make sure there's enough detail for the AI to work
  with and for other educators to learn from. A few sentences each will easily
  clear the bar.
</Callout>

## If you have a solution

If you started from **"I have a solution"** (the **"Share case"** button), the form adds a **Solution** section. Here you describe:

- **"Detailed description of the solution"** — what you actually did.
- **"Outcome of the solution"** — what happened as a result.
- A **success rating** for how it went.

This is what turns your case into something others can learn from directly.

## Save or publish

When you're done, you have two buttons:

- **"Save"** — keeps the case as a private draft, visible only to you (and administrators).
- **"Save and publish"** — saves it and shares it with the community right away.

Either way, _"The created case study can be found in your profile."_ You can publish a private case (or unpublish a shared one) later from there.

## The AI still helps

Even on the text path, you don't have to write everything. If you don't supply a title, the AI **automatically generates a title and summary** for your case from what you wrote. Your case is also detected as Czech or English and translated so it's available in both languages.

<Callout type="info">
  Unlike the voice path, there's no transcript to review — your case goes
  straight into your profile once saved.
</Callout>
