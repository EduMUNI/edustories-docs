Beyond the [AI-recommended solutions](/en/case-reports/ai-solutions), Edustories can turn a messy real-world situation into a **formal education plan** — a structured support document of the kind schools use officially. Three plan types are available, and they appear as their own tabs within the AI solutions section of a case.

## The three plans

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="BIP — Behavior Intervention Plan">
A **Behavior Intervention Plan** is a structured, written plan for addressing a specific challenging behaviour: what the behaviour is, the strategies to reduce it, and how progress is checked. It's a common US-style behaviour-support document. **Generated in English.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="IEP — Individualized Education Program">
  An **Individualized Education Program** is a comprehensive special-education
  plan tailored to one student — covering present levels of performance, goals,
  services, and accommodations. It's the cornerstone document of US special
  education. **Generated in English.**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="PLPP — Plán pedagogické podpory (Pedagogical Support Plan)">
A **Plán pedagogické podpory** is the Czech pedagogical support plan: a formal record of the support measures a school will provide to a student who needs them. **Generated in Czech.**
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Quick reference

| Plan     | Full name                                           | Language |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| **BIP**  | Behavior Intervention Plan                          | English  |
| **IEP**  | Individualized Education Program                    | English  |
| **PLPP** | Plán pedagogické podpory (Pedagogical Support Plan) | Czech    |

<Callout type="info">
  Because of how each plan is used, **BIP and IEP appear in English** and **PLPP
  appears in Czech**. Which plans you see depends on the case and your interface
  language.
</Callout>

## Finding and using them

The plans show up as tabs inside the **"AI-powered solution designing"** section of a case, alongside the other approaches. Like the rest of a case, you can include them when you [export to Word](/en/case-reports/exporting), giving you an editable document you can adapt and use in your own setting.
