Your profile is your **personal workspace for the case reports you have created** — not a public social page. Other people do not browse it; what they see of you is limited to the authorship shown on your published case reports.

At the top you will see your name, your professions and years of **Experience**, and the month you have been **On Edustories since**.

## Your case reports

Below the header, **Your Cases** lists every case report you have created, with a search box and an **Upload case** button to start a new one. Each case shows its date, name, tags, a behaviour-trend indicator, its status, and an actions menu.

Each case carries a status badge:

- **Private** — a draft, visible only to you.
- **Published** — visible to everyone in the shared case library.

## Publishing and unpublishing

From a case's actions menu you can switch it between private and public:

- **Publish** — makes the case visible to everyone in the shared library. Anyone browsing Edustories can find, read, and learn from it.
- **Unpublish** — returns the case to private, removing it from the shared library.

<Callout type="info">
  Before you publish, make sure the case is fully anonymized — no student names,
  school names, or places. Publishing makes the case available to all Edustories
  users.
</Callout>

For more on the full lifecycle of a case — from draft through review to published — see the case-report statuses guide.

## Exporting a case to Word

From a generated case's actions menu you can choose **Download to .docx** to export it as a Word document. The export modal lets you pick which sections and solution approaches to include, so you can take a tidy, printable version of the case away with you.
