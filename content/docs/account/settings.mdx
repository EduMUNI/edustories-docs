**Settings** has two sections in its side menu: **Profile** and **Account**.

## Settings → Profile

Here you manage how you appear and a few personal details:

- **Personal data** — your title before and after your name, **Name** and **Surname**, **Date of Birth** (optional), and your **Country**.
- **Experience** — your professions (you can select several, plus an "Other" free-text option) and your **Experience years**.
- **Interface language** — note that your interface language is chosen from the global language switcher (the flag menu in the header), not on this screen.

### Display name in case reports

The **"Display name in case reports"** option controls whether your name appears on the cases you publish.

- **On** — your real name is shown as the author on case reports you publish.
- **Off** — your case reports are published **anonymously**, with no name attached.

This option is **off by default**, so unless you turn it on, your published cases appear anonymously. It affects only authorship on case reports; it does not change anything else about your account.

## Settings → Account

The Account section lets you manage your sign-in details:

- **Email** — your account email address.
- **Change password** — enter your current password and a new one. For your security, the new password must differ from the current one, and the change only goes through if your current password is correct.

### Danger zone: deleting your account

At the bottom of the Account section, the **Danger zone** holds the **Delete
account** button. Deleting your account is **irreversible**: it permanently
removes the account and the data associated with it — your stories including
their comments, ratings and interventions, your conversations with the AI
assistant, your trainings and simulator sessions, and simulator groups you own.
You confirm the deletion with your password and an explicit "I understand that
this is irreversible" checkbox.

<Callout type="info">
  **The AI solution "lenses"** — proactive, reactive, SEL, PBIS, restorative
  practices, and nonviolent communication — are chosen by the system per case
  based on what fits the situation, not toggles you set here. See [AI
  solutions](/en/case-reports/ai-solutions) for how they appear on a case.
</Callout>
