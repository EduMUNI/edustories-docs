---
title: During a session
description: What happens inside a live training session — chatting with an AI character, per-message skill scoring, text-to-speech replies, and the final overall summary.
---

A training session is a live, typed conversation with an AI character. You write
messages, the character replies in character, and each of your messages is scored
in real time on the skills you chose.

## The conversation

The session opens with the AI character's first message, setting the scene. You
type your reply in the message box (**"Type your response here"**) and send it.
The character — labelled **Teacher**, **Student**, or **Parent** — replies in
character, and may even describe non-verbal behaviour, such as looking you in the
eye or smiling.

Everything happens **live**: replies and scores arrive on their own, in real time,
without you needing to refresh the page. While the AI is composing a reply you'll
see a typing indicator; if a reply is slow to arrive, a **Refresh** button appears
as a fallback.

<Callout type="info">
  AI character replies can be **read aloud**. The platform generates spoken
  audio for the character's messages, so you can listen as well as read.
</Callout>

## How each message is scored

Right after you send a message, an AI evaluator looks at **only that message** and
decides which of your chosen skills you actually demonstrated. For each one it
gives:

- a score from **0 to 3** (shown as "X/3"), and
- a one-line **reason** explaining the score, written directly to you.

A few things worth knowing:

- The evaluator only scores a skill it can clearly see you used. A skill you
  didn't use simply isn't listed.
- If **none** of your chosen skills appear in a message, that message shows
  **"Not rated"**.
- A coloured badge summarises each message; tap it to read the reasoning behind
  the score.

## Reaching the end

The session is **solved** once you reach enough successful skill uses for your
chosen difficulty (3 for Easy, 5 for Medium, 7 for Hard). At that point the
message box is replaced with **"The training was closed"** and an **Overall
Score** summary opens automatically:

- a **total points** figure and an overall percentage,
- a **Skills Breakdown** — a progress bar and percentage for each skill, and
- an AI-written **"What went well"** and **"What to improve"** summary, distilled
  from all your per-message feedback.

While the final summary is being prepared, you'll briefly see **"Analyzing your
performance…"**. When it's ready, **Back to overview** returns you to the Training
home.

<Callout type="warn">
  If you leave a session before it's solved, you lose your current answers and
  can't continue it — you'll be warned with **"Do you really want to leave the
  training?"** before leaving.
</Callout>
