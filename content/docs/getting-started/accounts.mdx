---
title: Creating an account & signing in
description: How to register for Edustories, complete the required Profile Setup, sign in, stay signed in, and sign out.
---

To write case reports, save items, rate, and comment, you need an Edustories account. Browsing the public library is open to everyone, but the personal tools require signing in.

## Registering

Creating an account is quick — you provide an email and a password and accept the terms.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the **Register** page and fill in your **Email** and a **Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Tick the box to agree with the **application usage terms** (this is
    required).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Submit the form. Your account is created and you are signed in straight
    away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  There is **no email-verification step**. You can start using Edustories
  immediately after registering — there's no confirmation email to wait for.
</Callout>

If you enter an email that already has an account, you'll be told **"Account with this email already exists"**. If you already have an account, use the **Login** link instead.

## Profile Setup (first time only)

The first time you sign in after registering, a short **Profile Setup** appears with the note _"Before we start, we need a few details"_. It has **two steps** and must be completed before you continue.

1. **Profession & experience** — Choose one or more professions (for example _special educator_, _teacher_, _teaching assistant_, _psychologist_), or use the **Other** option to type your own. At least one profession is required. Also enter your **years of experience**.
2. **Personal data & country** — Enter your name (with optional titles before and after), an optional date of birth, and your **country** (required).

You can change all of these later in your profile settings. See [Settings](/en/account/settings).

## Signing in

Returning users sign in on the **Login** page with their **Email** and **Password**, then select **Login**. If the details don't match, you'll see a single **"Invalid email or password"** message for security.

You may also be prompted to log in via a pop-up when you try an action that requires an account — for example rating, saving, or commenting on a case report.

## Staying signed in

Once you're signed in, Edustories keeps your session alive for you. Your sign-in **refreshes automatically in the background**, so you normally stay logged in across visits for up to 30 days without re-entering your password. If your session can't be renewed (for example after a long time away), you'll simply be asked to log in again.

## Signing out

To end your session, use **Log out**. This signs you out everywhere and clears your saved session on the device. If you were on a personal page at the time, you'll be returned to the home page.

## Deleting your account

<Callout type="info">
  Account deletion is handled **on request**, not as a self-service button. To
  have your account deleted, contact the project as described in the Terms of
  Use (including the Data Protection Officer contact). See [Privacy and your
  data](/en/account/privacy-and-data).
</Callout>
