Welcome to the Edustories documentation.

Edustories is a platform for writing, structuring, and sharing **educational
case reports** — with AI-assisted extraction from voice and text,
collaboration, and export to formats like DOCX.

This site is where product guides and reference material will live. It is
just getting started, so expect it to grow over time.
