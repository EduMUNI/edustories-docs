title: Welcome to Edustories

---

**Edustories** helps educators and special-education professionals turn real
classroom experiences into structured, shareable **case reports** —
and learn from a growing library of them.

You can describe a situation by voice or in writing, and Edustories helps
organise it, suggests evidence-based ways to handle the challenging behaviour,
finds similar cases, lets you practise your skills, and discuss everything with
an AI assistant.

These guides explain how each part works, in plain language — no technical
background needed.
